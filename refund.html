Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy applies to all fees paid to Birdie Squads for meetups, sessions and events ("Event"). By booking a spot, you agree to this policy along with our Terms & Conditions.

1. Cancellation by Participant

  • 24 hours or more before the Event: full refund, or credit towards a future Event if you prefer.
  • Less than 24 hours before the Event: no refund. You may transfer your spot to a friend by informing us in advance.
  • No-show: no refund or credit.

2. Cancellation by Birdie Squads

If we cancel or reschedule an Event (for example due to court unavailability, weather, low turnout or unforeseen circumstances), you can choose either a full refund or a spot at the rescheduled Event.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Leaving an Event early or arriving late.
  • Removal from an Event due to a breach of our Code of Conduct.
  • Injury or illness during the Event (fees cover court and event costs already incurred).

4. How to Request a Refund

Message us on WhatsApp (+91 63663 76397) or email bhavyabsquare@gmail.com with your name, phone number, Event date and payment reference.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account). Bank processing times may vary.
