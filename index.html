FAQ

Do I need to be good at badminton?

Not at all! Birdie Squad is for all levels. Fun and friendship come first.

When are the meetups?

Every weekend. Follow @birdie.squads on Instagram for dates, timings and venue.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sportswear, non-marking shoes, a water bottle, and a racquet if you have one.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely — most people do! Mix & match games make it easy to meet everyone.
