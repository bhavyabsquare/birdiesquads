Shipping & Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. No Physical Shipping

Birdie Squads provides in-person badminton social sessions. We do not sell or ship any physical products.

2. Service Delivery

  • After successful payment, your booking confirmation with session date, time and venue is sent via WhatsApp, SMS or email within 24 hours (usually immediately).
  • The service is delivered in person at the venue on the booked date and time, in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
  • If you do not receive a confirmation within 24 hours, contact us at bhavyabsquare@gmail.com or +91 63663 76397.

3. Changes & Cancellations

Any change in venue or timing will be communicated in advance. For cancellations and refunds, see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
